## Tasks

### Task 1: Billing & Invoicing Suite Redesign
- Redesign `/billing/invoices`:
  * Scorecard metrics: Total Billed, Outstanding, Overdue, Paid this month.
  * Filter pills (All, Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue).
  * Invoices data table with status pill, payment link copy button, and action menu.
- Redesign `/billing/invoices/new`:
  * Modern multi-line invoice builder: Client picker, currency/tax selectors, dynamic item table with live subtotal/tax/discount calculations.
  * Sticky bottom action bar on mobile (`Save as Draft`, `Send Invoice`).
- Redesign `/billing/quotes` & `/billing/quotes/new`:
  * Proposal status tracker, valid-until warning pills, and quote-to-invoice converter button.
- Redesign `/billing/subscriptions`:
  * Active subscriber count, MRR card, subscription cards with renewal badges.
- Redesign `/billing/statements`:
  * Date range selector, running balance scorecard, credit/debit color-coding.

### Task 2: Products & Inventory Catalog Overhaul
- Redesign `/products`:
  * Product grid / list toggle, category badges, pricing cards.
  * Search and inventory stock warning badges.
- Redesign `/inventory/products`:
  * Stock level progress bars, low-stock alerts, SKU table, stock adjustment dialog.

### Task 3: Marketing & Automation Overhaul
- Redesign `/marketing` & `/marketing/ads`:
  * Campaign performance scorecards, audience segmentation lists, email broadcast cards.
- Redesign `/automations`:
  * Active triggers list, condition pills, execution history log table.
- Redesign `create-rule-dialog.tsx`:
  * Visual trigger and action selection modal with clear contrast steps.
